Demo clock

Fixed at 19 October 2026, 9 PM IST initially. It does not advance automatically. The background worker checks every 15 seconds; demo time moves only when you change it.

What actually runs?

SQLite persistence, ingestion deduplication, evidence extraction, review gating, revision reconciliation, in-app reminder scheduling and reply drafts. Offline extraction/planning are rule-based baselines. Optional model adapters supply proposals to the same two roles, never autonomous send actions.

EvidenceExtractor → human date review → ActionPlanner → deterministic reminder worker.
